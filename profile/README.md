# Scan Global Logistics

### Uncomplicating the world of logistics, every day.

We are a freight forwarder — air, ocean, rail, road, contract logistics and
project cargo — run by 5,100+ people across 200+ offices on six continents,
from our head office in Hvidovre, Denmark.

This organisation is where our internal engineering happens: the platforms and
tools our own colleagues use to move freight, clear customs, screen
counterparties and see what is going on.

## What we build

- **Internal platforms** — self-service hosting for the sites, dashboards and
  applications teams build for themselves, with single sign-on, per-team
  isolation and identity-correct data access built in rather than bolted on.
- **Compliance and trade** — screening and control systems that keep shipments
  and counterparties on the right side of sanctions and export rules.
- **Data and analytics** — a governed platform over our operational systems:
  modelling, pipelines and reporting that operations actually run on.
- **Security engineering** — endpoint health, vulnerability management and
  detection tooling across a global fleet.
- **Digital workplace** — intranet, collaboration and integration work that
  connects the tools people use every day.

## How we work

- **One issue, one branch, one pull request.** Work starts as a tracked issue.
  Nothing reaches `main` any other way.
- **Green, or it does not merge.** CI runs the suite on every pull request, and
  the branch has to be up to date with `main` before it lands. The rules bind
  admins too — they are enforced by the platform, not by good intentions.
- **Agents write, people review.** We build with AI coding agents as a matter
  of course, and everything an agent writes is read by a human before it
  merges. The review is the point, not a formality.
- **Documentation ships with the change.** A user-facing change updates its own
  guidance in the same pull request.

## Why there is nothing to browse here

Our repositories are private. We build for our own colleagues rather than for a
public user base, so this organisation hosts no open-source projects today and
does not take unsolicited external contributions.
